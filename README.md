# The Vital Fuel: Human Nutrition & Nutrient Health Explorer 🥑🧪

An interactive, scientific, client-side reference application exploring essential human macronutrients, all 13 human vitamins, 13 essential minerals, daily recommended intake (RDA/DRI/AI), whole food resources with authentic photography, dynamic meal telemetry, and bioavailability synergies.

Part of the **Gamility Interactive Explorer Hub** collection.

---

## ✨ Features

- **30+ Comprehensive Nutrients**:
  - **4 Macronutrient Classes**: Complete Protein & Essential Amino Acids, Complex Carbohydrates & Fiber, Healthy Fats & Omega-3/6/9, Hydration & Electrolytes.
  - **All 13 Human Vitamins**: Fat-Soluble (A, D3, E, K1/K2) and Water-Soluble (C, B1 Thiamine, B2 Riboflavin, B3 Niacin, B5 Pantothenic Acid, B6 Pyridoxine, B7 Biotin, B9 Folate, B12 Cobalamin).
  - **13 Essential Macrominerals & Trace Elements**: Calcium, Magnesium, Potassium, Phosphorus, Sodium, Iron (Heme & Non-Heme), Zinc, Iodine, Selenium, Copper, Manganese, Chromium, Molybdenum.
- **60+ Real Whole Food Resources**:
  - Photorealistic high-resolution photography across fruits, vegetables, seeds, nuts, legumes, seafood, eggs, and grains.
  - Serving portion metrics (per 100g and per standard portion), nutrient concentration, % Daily Value (% DV), and dietary flags (Vegan, Vegetarian, Pescatarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Low-Carb).
- **Personalized RDA / DRI Engine**:
  - Dynamic calculator calibrated to NIH Dietary Reference Intakes by Age, Biological Sex, Physical Activity, Life Stage (Pregnancy/Lactation), and Dietary Pattern.
- **Interactive Daily Plate / Meal Builder**:
  - Build your day's meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks) and track live fulfillment gauges for Calories, Protein, Fiber, Iron, Calcium, Magnesium, Vitamin C, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, and Zinc with deficiency (<50%) and toxicity (>UL) alerts.
- **Nutrient Synergy & "Power Pairs" Matrix**:
  - Visual chemistry guide to absorption enhancers (Iron + Vitamin C; Calcium + D3 + K2; Turmeric + Piperine; Lipids + Fat-Soluble Vitamins) and competing inhibitors.
- **Symptom & Deficiency Diagnostic Guide**:
  - Search physical symptoms (fatigue, muscle spasms, brain fog, brittle nails, bleeding gums) to uncover underlying micronutrient pathways and dietary replenishment foods.
- **Nutritional Mastery Quiz**:
  - Multi-tier gamified challenge with procedural Web Audio sound feedback.
- **100% Client-Side Privacy**:
  - Zero external database calls, zero tracking cookies, zero ads. Works offline.
